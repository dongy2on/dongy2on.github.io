Cryptographic Research

A Study on Image Encryption System Based on Tetration Operation

2025. 67th Gyeongnam Science Exhibition Entry  ·  슈퍼쏘니 (Dongyeon Kwon, Sihyeon Kim, Yeseo Ahn)
1. Motivation and Purpose

In modern society, information security has become a critical pillar of daily life and state infrastructure. However, with the rapid advance of computational power, traditional encryption methods are increasingly vulnerable to brute-force attacks. This research proposes a revolutionary approach to image cryptography by leveraging Tetration—a hyper-operation that scales vastly faster than normal exponentiation.

The core objective of this study is to utilize the chaotic behavior, extreme sensitivity to initial conditions, and mathematical non-invertibility of tetration to develop a highly secure, next-generation encryption algorithm for digital assets.

2. Research Methods & Core Mechanisms

A. Design of Tetration-Based Algorithm
Tetration (iterated exponentiation) generates numbers that grow at astronomical rates. By applying tetration sequences to image pixel grids, we constructed a dynamic cipher capable of scrambling pixel positions and altering data values simultaneously.

B. Multi-Layered Diffusion & Confusion
The encryption process executes repetitive mathematical operations where convergence and divergence patterns are treated as security parameters. Even with identical source images, using slightly different computational keys produces completely independent ciphertexts.

C. High-Precision Key Optimization
Because tetration calculations are highly chaotic, the algorithm enforces ultra-high-precision floating-point operations to prevent decryption errors while guaranteeing that a microscopic change in the key space results in an unresolvable avalanche effect.

3. Results & Expected Contributions

A. Cryptographic Performance Analysis
Empirical testing demonstrated that executing the tetration algorithm for 10 iterations transforms structural images into completely chaotic, randomized visual noise. Due to the lack of an analytical inverse function for tetration, the cipher is immune to standard reverse-engineering attacks, exponentially maximizing data resistance against brute-force intrusion.

B. Future Outlook and Impact
This research successfully shifts the paradigm of encryption from exponential complexity to hyper-operational complexity. Although requiring substantial computational overhead for high-precision matrix processing, its superior security thresholds make it exceptionally viable for high-stakes governmental, medical, and military data protection.
